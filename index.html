FAQ

Questions singers ask

How do I find the right key to sing a song in?

Compare the song's highest and lowest melody notes with the range you can sing comfortably, then move the song up or down by the difference. PitchPilot Pro does this for you: sing for a few seconds, and it detects your vocal key and range and tells you exactly how many semitones to transpose the song on screen.

Is there a Chrome extension that transposes chords automatically?

Yes. PitchPilot detects the key of the song on supported chord sites and transposes every chord in one click, without changing the lyrics. Transposing is free.

Which chord sites does PitchPilot work on?

Ultimate Guitar, Chordify, Songsterr, MuseScore, Cifra Club, E-Chords, Chordie, Tabs4Acoustic, Genius, Tab4U, Negina, Nagnu and Shironet.

Can I transpose a song down if a capo only goes up?

Yes. PitchPilot rewrites the chords themselves, so you can move a song down or up by any number of semitones.

Does PitchPilot record or upload my voice?

No. Audio is processed on your device and never uploaded. No account is needed.
