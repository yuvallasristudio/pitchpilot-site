Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

PitchPilot ("the Extension") is a browser extension that analyzes your vocal range and helps you transpose songs. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

  • Microphone audio: Processed locally in your browser in real time to detect pitch. Audio is never recorded, stored, or transmitted to any server.
  • License key: Stored locally in your browser's storage to validate your purchase. Validated against LemonSqueezy's API servers.
  • Extension settings: Theme preference, feature toggles, and voice-only mode are stored locally in your browser.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personal information such as your name or email address.
  • We do not track your browsing history or the pages you visit.
  • We do not store or transmit your voice or audio data.
  • We do not use cookies or analytics.

Third-Party Services

License key validation is handled by LemonSqueezy. When you activate or validate a license key, your key is sent to LemonSqueezy's API. Please refer to LemonSqueezy's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle that data.

Permissions

  • Microphone: Used solely for real-time pitch detection. No audio is recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Active Tab: Used to read chord information from the current page and apply transpositions.
  • Storage: Used to save your settings and license key locally on your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
